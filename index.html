Agentic Multi-Robot Orchestration in Image-Guided Interventions

Research question

When two robots share a crowded interventional workspace that is only partially known, how should an agent decide whether to replan robot motion or first acquire new observations of the scene?

My contribution

I built an Isaac Sim digital twin of transpedicular access in which a Brainlab Loop-X must reach prescribed X-ray imaging views while sharing the workspace with a UR5 needle-guide arm. I developed a language-model agent that could replan robot motion or acquire new stereo RGB observations from the Loop-X to update the digital twin, combining motion planning, stereo reconstruction, and inter-robot registration.

Result

Across 18 simulated interference scenarios, the agent reached all target poses without collision in 18/18 scenarios with ground-truth geometry and in 14/18 with the twin reconstructed from stereo RGB observations. A rule-based coordinator succeeded in 12/18 under both conditions. Co-first-authored manuscript under review at IPCAI 2027.

Understanding and Shaping Generative AI Use

Research question

Do students accurately understand how they use generative AI, and can personalized feedback help them change that behavior?

My contribution

I built a macOS client and Go backend that measure token use in ChatGPT Work mode on macOS via OpenTelemetry, keeping minute-level records on the device and uploading only encrypted daily summaries. A dashboard compares each student's daily use with their own baseline.

Status

The measurement system and dashboard are built; the user study is planned. I drafted an IRB protocol for design interviews and a planned 28-day within-participant study in which each participant's feedback would begin on a randomly assigned day.

A Social Robot for Natural Café Interaction

Research question

How can a café robot support natural, socially engaging interaction rather than serve only as an ordering interface?

My contribution

I built and deployed the GPT-4o conversational pipeline with intent recognition, visual emotion monitoring, Whisper speech recognition, speech synthesis, and ROS 2 control, and led two rounds of iterative co-design.

Result

Refined the robot’s dialogue policy over two co-design rounds with the same two participants. In the second round, the robot made fewer unsolicited drink suggestions and sustained conversations on topics beyond coffee.

ARCADE LAB · MEDICAL ROBOTICS01

Automated Pedicle Screw Trajectory Planning

I designed and implemented an automated planning pipeline for T1–L5 (17 vertebral levels) and generated more than 23,000 candidate trajectories from 742 CT-derived spine models.

Result91.7% planning success · 99.3% validation pass · 0% endplate violations · 2.5–3.3 mm median cortical clearance

ARCADE LAB · GENERATIVE IMAGING02

Predicting X-ray Views Before C-arm Motion

I built an observation-prediction module that combines the current fluoroscopic image with a candidate six-degree-of-freedom C-arm motion, using a U-Net with cross-attention and pose-conditioned adaptive normalization in DDPM and DDIM frameworks.

Technical output827 CT volumes · 100 imaging poses per case · approximately 1,500 training pairs per case
