I am a Master's student in Computer Science at Johns Hopkins 
University, working in the [ARCADE Lab](https://www.arcade.cs.jhu.edu/) 
advised by Prof. [Mathias Unberath](https://mathiasunberath.github.io/) 
and Ph.D. [Blanca Inigo Romillo](https://binigoromillo.github.io/). 
My research focuses on Medical Robotics and AI-Assisted Intervention, 
including the application of deep learning, diffusion models, and 
vision-language models to surgical perception and planning, as well 
as multi-agent systems for coordinating complex clinical workflows.
